My name is Narutatsu, but please feel free to call me Edward or Tatsu. I am a PhD student at Princeton University in the Computer Science Department and Princeton Language + Intelligence (PLI), where I am fortunate to be advised by Professor Sanjeev Arora. I also work closely with Professor Micah Goldblum at Columbia University. I am gratefully supported by the Gordon Wu Fellowship and the Ezoe Memorial Recruit Foundation. Please find my CV here.

Research Interests: I'm broadly interested in AI safety (e.g., developing a scientific understanding of failure modes in current models), misalignment (e.g., how scaling capabilities enables undesirable behavior in frontier models), and self-improvement, along with the safety implications that come with it.

I previously finished my B.S./M.S. at Columbia University where I was part of the Egleston Scholars Program. I am indebted to Professors Kathleen McKeown, Daniel Hsu, and Nakul Verma, by whom I have had the great fortune of being advised.
